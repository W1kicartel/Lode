# Lode glossary: English

How Lode's words are said in English. Register: plain international English for students anywhere, British spelling (colour, organise, programme for a TV show but *syllabus* for an exam), informal «you», short sentences. Commands in quotes are the ones the English recognizer understands (`js/comandi/en.js`).
